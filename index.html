Frequently Asked Questions

Are bats, stumps, and footballs included in the booking?

Basic stumps and goalposts are always included. You can add extra tennis balls, bats, or team bibs while filling out the booking form.

What footwear is allowed on the artificial turf?

Only flat-sole sports shoes or turf studs are permitted. Metal spikes and formal shoes are strictly prohibited to protect the grass.

How does the bill split work for college groups?

Check our per-player split chart in the Slots & Pricing section. The team captain pays a ₹200 advance to lock the slot and collects the equal share from each player.
